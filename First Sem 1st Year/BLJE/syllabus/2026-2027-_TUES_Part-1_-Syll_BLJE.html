BASIC LEGAL AND JUDICIAL ETHICS

Tuesday, 5:30 pm – 8:30 pm

A.Y. 2026-2027 / Term 2

Atty. Leny Santos Sanchez

S Y L L A B U S

(1 st Part)

COURSE DESCRIPTION

1. LEGAL ETHICS- the course covers the duties that a lawyer owes to the public, to the

court, to his or her clients, and to his or her colleagues in the legal profession as

embodied in the CPRA, the Constitution, the Rules of Court, laws, and jurisprudence.

2. JUDICIAL ETHICS- the course covers the proper conduct to be observed by the

members of the judiciary in resolving or deciding legal controversies.

COURSE OBJECTIVES

1. To be able to appreciate the value of the legal profession;

2. To develop a clear comprehension of the meaning and significance of justice, fairness,

candor, honesty, integrity, professionalism, and respect for the rule of law, and the

implication of the foregoing in adhering to ethical standards and practices;

3. To equip the law students with various materials that will guide them in fully

understanding the basic principles of Legal and Judicial Ethics.

COURSE REQUIREMENTS

1. Obtain a passing grade in the class standing, which includes oral recitation and group

activities (research, case digest, group reporting);

2. Obtain passing grades in the midterm and final examinations.

GRADING SYSTEM

PASSING GRADE: 3.00 or 75%

➢CLASS STANDING: 25%(recit, reporting, and activities)

➢MIDTERM EXAM: 35%

➢FINAL EXAM : 40%

ATTENDANCE: 6 Absences: automatic "FAILED" mark

NOTES FOR THE CLASS BEADLE

a. Kindly check the attendance at 6:00 pm.

b. Assign a student to say the prayer every meeting.

LEARNING ACTIVITIES

1. SOCRATIC METHOD OF LEARNING – a form of cooperative argumentative dialogue

between individuals, based on asking and answering questions to stimulate critical

thinking and to draw out ideas and underlying presumptions.

CLASS POLICIES

1. Zoom Class Attire: corporate or smart casual.

2. Camera ON.

3. Put your AUDIO ON MUTE unless you are being asked to speak/recite.

4. In reciting cases, limit the discussion to the following:

a. Relevant facts (brief discussion only)

b. Pertinent issue/s

c.

Legal basis

d. Ruling of the Supreme Court in relation to the assigned topic/s);

5. Quizzes may be given unannounced.

6. Respect each student's individuality, religion, political views, sexual orientation, and

beliefs.

7. We will try to extensively cover the assigned topics every meeting. Please note that

the syllabus will not be adjusted, notwithstanding class suspensions. The coverage

for the midterm and final examinations will remain unchanged, even if some of the

assigned topics have not been fully covered or discussed before the respective

examination dates.

April 11, 2023;
