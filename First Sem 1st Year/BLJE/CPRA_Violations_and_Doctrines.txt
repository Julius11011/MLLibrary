================================================================================
DOCTRINES AND CANONS OF THE CODE OF PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY AND ACCOUNTABILITY (CPRA)
                    IN PHILIPPINE LEGAL AND LAW ETHICS
================================================================================
Effective Date: May 29, 2023 (A.M. No. 22-09-01-SC)
Replaces the 1988 Code of Professional Responsibility (CPR)

This list summarizes the 6 main Canons of the CPRA, the key rules/sections 
commonly violated in Philippine administrative/disciplinary cases, and the 
established legal doctrines associated with each violation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CANON I: INDEPENDENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mandate: A lawyer shall maintain independence in the discharge of professional 
duties, free from improper influence, pressure, or interference.

1. Rule 1.01 - Independence from Extraneous Influences
   * Violation: Allowing personal, financial, social, political, or other 
     extraneous interests to influence professional judgment.
   * Doctrine: A lawyer's professional judgment must be free from any double-dealing 
     or personal interests that conflict with their client's cause.

2. Rule 1.03 - Non-involvement in Illegal/Unethical Acts
   * Violation: Assisting or counseling a client in activities known to be 
     illegal, fraudulent, or deceitful.
   * Doctrine: A lawyer's first and highest duty is to the law and the administration 
     of justice, which supersedes the duty to the client.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CANON II: PROPRIETY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mandate: A lawyer shall, at all times, act with propriety and maintain the 
appearance of propriety in both personal and professional dealings.

1. Section 1 - Propriety in Personal and Professional Conduct
   * Violation: Grossly immoral conduct, dishonesty, or indecent behavior.
   * Doctrine: Good moral character is not only a condition precedent to bar admission 
     but a continuing requirement. Conduct showing moral turpitude, even in a 
     lawyer's private life (such as sexual exploitation of a minor or extramarital 
     affairs), warrants disbarment or suspension (e.g., Ventura v. Samson, Guevarra v. Eala).

2. Section 2 - Respect for the Courts, Colleagues, and Parties
   * Violation: Using disrespectful, abusive, or offensive language in court pleadings; 
     harassing opposing counsel.
   * Doctrine: Lawyers must treat opposing counsel and the court with professional courtesy, 
     dignity, and respect. Poking fun at, mocking, or filing retaliatory suits 
     against opposing counsel violates legal ethics (e.g., Cabarroguis v. Basa).

3. Section 28 - Responsible Use of Social Media
   * Violation: Posting misogynistic, sexist, profane, or abusive content online.
   * Doctrine: A lawyer's speech on social media is subject to ethical regulation. 
     Using online platforms to hurl profanities or engage in gender-based harassment 
     degrades the legal profession and is ground for disbarment (e.g., Re: Lorenzo Gadon).

4. Section 37 - Prohibition on Romantic or Sexual Relationship with a Client
   * Violation: Entering into a romantic, dating, or sexual relationship with a client 
     during the professional engagement.
   * Doctrine: Dating or having a sexual relationship with a client creates an inherent 
     conflict of interest, compromises objective professional judgment, and exploits 
     the client's vulnerability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CANON III: FIDELITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mandate: A lawyer shall act with absolute fidelity, loyalty, and devotion to the 
client's cause, keeping client confidences inviolate.

1. Section 1 - Fiduciary Relationship & Confidentiality
   * Violation: Breaching client confidentiality or failing to protect prospective 
     client information.
   * Doctrine: A lawyer-client relationship (and the accompanying duty of confidentiality) 
     attaches the moment a prospective client consults the lawyer and shares 
     confidential details, regardless of whether a formal contract is signed or 
     fees are paid (e.g., Sison v. Dumlao, Legaspi v. Gonzales).

2. Section 6 - Conflict of Interest
   * Violation: Representing conflicting interests without written, informed consent 
     (e.g., representing both the landowner and illegal settler, or joint venture 
     partners with opposing interests).
   * Doctrine: A lawyer cannot represent conflicting interests as it violates the 
     duty of absolute loyalty and confidentiality owed to each client (e.g., 
     Buenavista Properties v. Deloria, Palma v. Maduramente).

3. Section 13 - Acquisition of Litigated Property
   * Violation: Leasing or buying property that is the direct object of active 
     litigation handled by the lawyer.
   * Doctrine: Under Article 1491 of the Civil Code and the CPRA, lawyers are strictly 
     prohibited from purchasing or leasing property under litigation by virtue of 
     their profession. This prevents exploitation of the fiduciary relationship 
     (e.g., Gabucan v. Narido).

4. Section 19 - Calling Client's Attention to Fraud
   * Violation: Assisting in or condoning a client's fraudulent acts (such as using 
     forged court orders).
   * Doctrine: If a lawyer discovers that a client has perpetrated a fraud upon a court 
     or person, the lawyer must immediately call on the client to rectify it. If the 
     client refuses, the lawyer must withdraw from representation (e.g., Tiongson v. Flores).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CANON IV: COMPETENCE AND DILIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mandate: A lawyer shall provide competent, diligent, and prompt legal services.

1. Section 1 - Competence and Diligent Representation
   * Violation: Neglecting a legal matter, failing to file pleadings on time, 
     missing pre-trial conferences, or letting a client be declared in default.
   * Doctrine: Acceptance of a case binds the lawyer to serve the client with competence 
     and warm zeal. Negligence in performing duties violates the lawyer's oath and 
     prejudices the client (e.g., Palma v. Maduramente).

2. Section 3 - Duty to Inform Client
   * Violation: Keeping the client in the dark; failing to provide updates; 
     misrepresenting the status of the case.
   * Doctrine: A lawyer has a fiduciary duty to keep the client reasonably informed. 
     Failing to do so, or fabricating spurious court orders to cover up inaction, 
     constitutes serious dishonesty (e.g., Manalang v. Buendia).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CANON V: EQUALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mandate: A lawyer shall treat all persons with equality, fairness, and respect, 
without bias or discrimination.

1. Section 1 - Non-discrimination
   * Violation: Using discriminatory, harassing, or biased language against any 
     person or group based on race, sex, gender, religion, or background.
   * Doctrine: The legal profession demands fairness and equal protection. Lawyers 
     must refrain from discriminatory actions and language that undermine equality 
     (e.g., Re: Lorenzo Gadon).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CANON VI: ACCOUNTABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mandate: A lawyer shall be accountable to society, the courts, and the legal 
profession for their actions, adhering to disciplinary procedures.

1. Section 32 & 33 - Abuse of Court Processes / Forum Shopping
   * Violation: Filing multiple frivolous, dilatory motions to delay execution of 
     final judgments; engaging in forum shopping.
   * Doctrine: Lawyers must assist in the speedy and efficient administration of 
     justice. Abusing procedural rules to frustrate final and executory decisions 
     is an ethical violation (e.g., David v. Rongcal, Genato v. Mallari).

2. Section 35 - Restrictions on Government Lawyers / Public Officials
   * Violation: Public officials engaging in the private practice of law where 
     prohibited, or practicing within the 1-year ban after separation.
   * Doctrine: Government employees and public officials who are lawyers are barred 
     from private practice (or face a 1-year ban in their former offices after resigning) 
     to prevent conflict of interest, influence peddling, and corruption 
     (e.g., Query of Atty. Silverio-Buffe).

3. Rule 138 (Rules of Court) - Unauthorized Practice of Law / Advertising
   * Violation: Publishing commercial advertisements offering legal services; 
     unauthorized practice by corporations.
   * Doctrine: The practice of law is a privilege reserved for individuals of good 
     moral character admitted to the Bar. It cannot be practiced by corporations or 
     advertised like merchant goods (e.g., Atty. Ulep v. Legal Clinic).
