CASE DIGEST

STATUTORY CONSTRUCTION

Case Title: ________________________________________________ G.R. No.: __________________________ Date: ______________________________ Ponente: ___________________________

I. FACTS OF THE CASE

State the material facts of the case necessary to understand the controversy and the ruling of the Court. Avoid including facts that are not relevant to the issues decided by the Court.

II. FACTS RELEVANT TO STATUTORY CONSTRUCTION

Identify the facts that gave rise to the need for the Court to construe, interpret, or determine the application of a law, rule, constitutional provision, or other legal provision.

In particular, explain:

What provision of law or rule became relevant?

What circumstance made the interpretation or construction of the provision necessary?

What competing interpretations or legal consequences were presented, if any?

III. ISSUES

A. General Legal Issues

State the principal legal issue/s decided by the Court.

B. Statutory-Construction Issue/s

State the specific issue/s involving the interpretation or construction of the law.

The issue should preferably be stated in the following form:

Whether __________________________ under Section _____ of __________________________ means/includes/applies to __________________________.

IV. PROVISION/S OF LAW CONSTRUED

Identify the statutory, constitutional, procedural, or other legal provision/s that the Court construed.

Provision:

Quote only the relevant portion of the provision.

Then briefly state:

What interpretive problem did the provision present?

V. RULING OF THE SUPREME COURT

A. General Ruling

State the Court's disposition of the case.

B. Ruling on the Statutory-Construction Issue

State specifically what meaning or interpretation the Court gave to the provision.

Do not merely state the result of the case. Explain what the Court held the law to mean and how that interpretation affected the outcome.

VI. HOW THE COURT CONSTRUED THE LAW

This is the most important part of the digest.

A. Why Was Construction Necessary?

Explain why the Court had to construe the provision.

Indicate, where applicable, whether the need for construction arose from:

ambiguity or uncertainty;

conflicting provisions;

an apparent inconsistency;

a gap or omission in the law;

a change in circumstances;

a subsequent amendment or repeal;

uncertainty as to legislative intent;

a literal interpretation producing an unreasonable or unjust result; or

another circumstance.

Explanation:

B. Purpose of Construction

Explain what purpose the Court sought to achieve through its interpretation.

Did the Court seek to:

ascertain legislative intent;

give effect to the purpose or spirit of the law;

avoid an absurd or unreasonable result;

promote justice;

harmonize conflicting provisions;

protect a constitutional right;

give effect to a remedial or social policy; or

achieve another purpose?

Explanation:

C. Method, Canon, or Rule of Construction Applied

Identify the specific canon, maxim, principle, or method of statutory construction relied upon or applied by the Court.

Examples include:

Verba legis non est recedendum

Absoluta sententia expositore non indiget

Ejusdem generis

Noscitur a sociis

Expressio unius est exclusio alterius

Casus omissus pro omisso habendus est

In pari materia

Ut res magis valeat quam pereat

strict construction

liberal construction

purposive construction

legislative intent

contemporaneous construction

constitutional construction

and other recognized rules or principles of statutory construction.

Canon/Method: ___________________________________________

How did the Court apply it?

What interpretation resulted from its application?

D. Other Sources Used by the Court in Construing the Law

Identify other sources or considerations used by the Court in determining the meaning of the provision, such as:

the ordinary meaning of the words;

the statute as a whole;

legislative intent;

legislative history;

purpose or policy of the law;

related statutes;

constitutional provisions;

previous Supreme Court decisions;

administrative interpretation;

historical circumstances;

contemporaneous circumstances;

consequences of a particular interpretation;

equity, justice, or fairness.

IX. THE CONSTRUCTION MADE BY THE COURT

In your own words, summarize the Court's actual construction of the law.

Complete the following:

The Court construed ______________________________ under Section ______ of ______________________________ to mean/apply/include/exclude ______________________________ because ______________________________.

Then explain briefly the principal reasoning by which the Court arrived at this construction.
