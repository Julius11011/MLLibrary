SUBJECT: 2024 DOJ-NPS RULES ON SUMMARY INVESTIGATION AND EXPEDITED PRELIMINARY INVESTIGATION

================================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================
Department Circular No. 028 (DC 28) establishes the "2024 DOJ-NPS Rules on Summary 
Investigation and Expedited Preliminary Investigation." These Rules govern criminal case 
investigations in the National Prosecution Service (NPS) for offenses where the law 
prescribes a penalty of six (6) years or below, a fine (regardless of amount), or both. 
It complements the regular investigation rules under DOJ Circular No. 015.

================================================================================
RULE I & II: TITLE, SCOPE, AND DUAL-TRACK SYSTEM
================================================================================
* Section 1. Title: "2024 DOJ-NPS Rules on Summary Investigation and Expedited Preliminary Investigation"
* Section 2. Scope: Applies to all crimes or offenses punishable by imprisonment of one (1) 
  day to six (6) years, a fine regardless of the amount, or both.
* The Dual-Track Investigation System:
  1. TRACK A: SUMMARY INVESTIGATION
     * Coverage: Imprisonment of one (1) day to one (1) year.
     * Procedure: Evaluated ex parte (based on the complainant's affidavits and evidence). 
       The prosecutor immediately recommends dismissal or prepares the Information.
     * Remedy Restriction: No Motion for Reconsideration is allowed.
  2. TRACK B: EXPEDITED PRELIMINARY INVESTIGATION
     * Coverage: Imprisonment of one (1) year and one (1) day to six (6) years.
     * Remedy Restriction: A Motion for Reconsideration is allowed, but must be filed 
       within ten (10) calendar days from receipt. No reply-affidavits/rejoinders are allowed.

================================================================================
QUANTUM OF EVIDENCE
================================================================================
* Quantum: Prima facie evidence with reasonable certainty of conviction (adopted from 
  DOJ Circular No. 015).
* Pro-active Case Build-up: Requires prosecutors to play a proactive role in directing 
  law enforcement during the investigation to ensure the case is trial-ready.

================================================================================
PROCEDURAL TIMELINE & WORKFLOW
================================================================================
* Investigation Period (Expedited Track): The investigating prosecutor must resolve the 
  complaint within twenty (20) calendar days from the receipt of the entire case records.
* Approval Period: The Prosecutor General, Provincial/City Prosecutor, or their authorized 
  deputies must act on the recommendatory resolution and information within five (5) 
  calendar days from receipt.
* Digital Filing: Institutionalizes e-filing and virtual hearings to accelerate the process.
